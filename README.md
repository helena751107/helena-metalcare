# Helena MetalCare — truly knowing my sister

![Helena MetalCare — care with the strength of metal](assets/social-preview.jpg)

> A record of getting to truly know my sister's schizophrenia before I die.
> "MetalCare" is a kept typo of "MentalCare" — care with the strength of metal:
> voices as audio, medication, a doctor's counsel, a patient's script.
>
> Part of the [**Helena Content Foundry**](https://github.com/helena751107/helena_phone) — mobile-first, MCP-verified.

---

## What's here

| Step | What |
|------|------|
| **Listening** | Reproducing the voices she hears as audio — "this is how it sounds to her", in real sound |
| **Analysis** | Studying the medication she takes |
| **Counsel** | Visiting a real clinic and consulting a specialist |
| **Mirroring** | Reading <A Beautiful Mind> as a mirror on schizophrenia (noting where the film fictionalizes) |
| **Script** | Setting a schizophrenia patient in a script (original fiction) |

## What this repo is

Not a finished "mental-care guide" — it's **the process of my coming to know**, itself.
A moving record, not a stopped conclusion. (Reproducing the thinking, not the result.)

## What I keep

- **Never expose my sister's identity** (name · location · photo).
- Voices, medication, and symptoms are **records for understanding, not spectacle.**
- **No medical assertion** — always defer to "a professional must confirm".
