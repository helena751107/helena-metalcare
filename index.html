04Levels

Levels · Steady / Worsening / Emergency

This is a household sketch. In an emergency, close this page and call the official number.

L0 · Steady

Steady

Routine is running. Prevention and record-keeping.

  • Check medication · meals · sleep
  • 1-line session card
  • Weekly community check-in
L1 · Strain

Worsening

Load is rising. Rest before schedule.

  • Scale back broadcasting/work
  • Share with the center / people close to you
  • Consider postponing administrative appointments
L2 · Crisis

Emergency

Go through official channels immediately. Stop content.

  • 119 / 112 / Mental health crisis line
  • Local mental health welfare center
  • Don't handle it alone
Emergency. If there is a risk of self-harm or harm to others, do not rely on this webzine — use 119 · 112 · your local mental health crisis hotline · the emergency room.
